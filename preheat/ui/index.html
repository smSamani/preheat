Model notes

Preheat simulates a heat-pump home over one synthetic day. The baseline meets the temperature requirements regardless of price. The optimised schedule minimises the electricity bill while respecting the same temperature limits.

Better insulation lowers the heat-loss coefficient from 0.20 to 0.13 kW/K. Both cases use the same heat pump. Evening usage covers 17:00–22:00. Daily cost includes all 24 hours.
